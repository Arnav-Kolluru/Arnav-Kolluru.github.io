Selected Research

Computational genomics research at the University of Alabama at Birmingham.

Physical Activity GWAS

First-author manuscript

Developed a post-GWAS analysis pipeline that expanded 117 lead variants into 3,599 candidate variants and prioritized 35 potentially functional regulatory variants across 13 loci. Integrated GTEx, ENCODE4, motifbreakR, and AlphaGenome to evaluate regulatory effects, including at MEF2C and PACS1/KLC2.

Parkinson’s Disease GWAS

Co-first-author manuscript

Analyzed 166 GWAS lead variants and 3,755 LD proxies, prioritizing 88 potentially functional variants across 46 loci. Combined brain-specific eQTL evidence, regulatory annotations, and sequence-based predictions to investigate signals involving CTSB, SNCA, GPNMB, and TMEM175.

Metabolic Syndrome GWAS

First-author manuscript

Expanded 1,650 lead variants into 50,478 variants and prioritized 698 candidates across 258 loci. Applied Borzoi and AlphaGenome across brain and metabolic tissues, and investigated tissue-sample-size bias using eQTL data, donor matching, and statistical normalization.
